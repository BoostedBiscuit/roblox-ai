# THE MIDNIGHT EYE · story, bible and look (Hollow Bay)

Original "In this town…" story built with the IN THIS TOWN master prompt. Vertical 9:16, about 2:00.
Format: Type B (survival / escalating danger). First person (Nara). Ending: complete payoff, no cliffhanger.
(The first version, "The Midnight Sea", is in the git history: commit 15463bbb and before.)

| Central rule | At midnight a giant Eye opens in the sky for one hour. Anyone who sees it walks into the sea and never comes back. |

Safe zones: inside with shutters closed and mirrors covered · under the pier (no sky, dry sand).
Unsafe zones: open sky · windows · wet sand · the water · black screens.
Rising costs: Old Bram taken → Nara's hand cut → Nara taken → stranded outside → the whale lost → Milo taken → Bram never comes back.

## Stage 2 · Script (APPROVED)

1. In this town, a giant Eye opens in the sky at midnight. If you see it… you walk into the sea.
2. Not just in the sky. In a window. A puddle. Even a black phone screen.
3. It stays open for one hour. The only way to stop someone: cover their eyes before the water reaches their knees.
4. Tonight, Old Bram forgot. He looked up from the pier… and walked straight into the red water.
5. Milo and I were home alone. Shutters closed. Mirrors covered. And Milo whispered what he always does: "I just want to see it once."
6. At twelve-oh-five, the wind ripped our shutter open and cracked the glass. I threw a blanket over it. It sliced my hand.
7. Then Milo opened the front door. His whale was still on the porch.
8. I dragged him back… and saw it. In the porch window. Staring at me.
9. My legs started walking. I couldn't stop them.
10. Milo squeezed his eyes shut… and followed the sound of my footsteps.
11. The water hit my ankles. My shins. Then two small hands covered my eyes.
12. I stopped. But we were outside, under the open sky. Forty minutes left.
13. We crawled to the pier, eyes shut. Milo let go of his whale to hold my hand. The tide took it.
14. Under the pier, my phone slipped out. Face up. A black mirror. Red light crawled across it… the Eye was looking for us.
15. "Close your eyes," I told Milo. I shut mine… and turned the screen to the sky.
16. The Eye saw itself. And for the first time… something else walked into the sea.
17. Then Milo's hand slipped out of mine. He never closed his eyes.
18. He finally saw it. And now he was following it into the water.
19. I couldn't look either. So I shut my eyes and ran toward his splashing. Just like he did for me.
20. The water reached my shins. On Milo, that's almost his knees. I grabbed his face and covered his eyes.
21. He stopped. The red drained from the water. We never got Old Bram back. Or the whale.
22. The next night, at midnight, we opened every shutter. And the sky stayed empty.
23. Milo doesn't ask to see it anymore.

## Stage 3 · Character and world bible (APPROVED)

All characters are original blocky Roblox-style avatars built in our own 3D pipeline (midnight-sea/cast.js). No real Roblox assets.

### Characters
**NARA VALE** (series protagonist, narrator, 15) — LOCKED LOOK, unchanged (same in every video):
large dark-brown tired eyes, faint dark circles, thin brows; long straight black hair in a low ponytail with a red hair tie;
oversized charcoal-grey hoodie (hood down, white drawstrings, kangaroo pocket), black jeans, black sneakers with white soles;
thin red string bracelet on the LEFT wrist. Personality: protective, stubborn, quietly brave.
- This story: her phone (black, cracked top-right corner) is in the hoodie pocket until line 14.
- Injury from line 6 on: a red cut across her RIGHT palm (visible in every later close-up of that hand).
- Emotional range: watchful → hurt → terrified (taken) → determined → desperate → relieved, wrecked.

**MILO VALE** (little brother, 8) — LOCKED LOOK, unchanged: child avatar about 0.8× Nara's height, big round eyes, freckles,
short messy black hair with a cowlick; red-and-white striped pyjamas, navy trousers, barefoot.
- The blue plush whale is in his LEFT hand from line 7 until line 13, when the tide takes it.
- Eyes: open at home; SHUT from line 10 to line 13; OPEN under the pier (he secretly keeps them open, line 15–17).
- Personality: curious, brave for his age, desperate to see the Eye "just once".
- Emotional range: curious → scared → brave (eyes shut, leading her) → taken (blank) → shaken and quiet.

**OLD BRAM** (fisherman, 60s) — LOCKED LOOK: grey beard, bushy grey brows, yellow raincoat over pale striped pyjama trousers,
dark boots. Tonight: on the pier at midnight with a fishing rod and a small lantern. He is the first victim (line 4).

**THE "TAKEN" LOOK** (anyone who sees the Eye: Bram, Nara, Milo): eyes wide open and unblinking, tiny pupils with a red glint,
blank face, arms slack, slow straight steps toward the sea. It stops the instant their eyes are covered.

(No sleepwalkers or dogs in this story: the rest of the town stays inside behind its shutters.)

### The Eye
The realistic eye from look test v3: veined white, deep-red iris, wet highlights, skin eyelids with lashes, a fleshy socket that fades
into the black sky; low over the sea, right of the pier. It opens at midnight and stays open one hour. While it is open the sea glows red
and its reflection shows in every reflective surface: window glass, puddles, wet sand, the water, black phone screens.
When it sees itself (line 16) its lids clench, it shudders, and it sinks down into the sea; the red drains out of the water (line 21).

### Locations
**NARA'S HOUSE** (the last house nearest the beach, pier side): two-storey fishing house, peeling dark paint.
- Ground-floor main room: front window with outside wooden shutters on a latch; a wall mirror covered with a white sheet; a small sofa,
  a low table, a dark grey wool blanket on the sofa; lights off, only thin lines of red light through the shutter cracks.
- Front door (dark wood) onto a small porch facing the sea; the PORCH WINDOW (bare glass) is just right of the door; Milo's whale
  sits on the porch boards. Two steps down to the street, then a short wooden stair down to the beach right below the house.

**THE BEACH**: black sand; a dark, glossy band of wet sand along the tide line (it mirrors the Eye); the pier on the left; three fishing
boats on the sand; the water shallow near the shore (ankles → shins → knees within a few steps).

**THE PIER**: the long wooden pier (deck on stilts). Old Bram stands near the far end at midnight; he walks off the end into the water and
his lantern rolls on the planks. UNDER THE PIER (the safe zone): dry sand, wet stilts with barnacles, no sky and no reflections;
they shelter behind the third stilt from the shore.

**THE NEXT NIGHT** (line 22–23): the same house, shutters wide open, a black empty sky, a calm black sea. No red anywhere.

### Props (continuity)
shutters (one torn open in line 6) · the cracked window · the dark grey blanket (stays over the window) · the white sheet on the mirror ·
Milo's blue whale (porch → his left hand → the tide) · Nara's phone (pocket → face up on the sand → in her right hand, screen to the sky) ·
Bram's fishing rod and lantern.

### Look (visual identity)
- The darker grade from the before/after: deep blacks, muted crimson mid-tones, highlights stay red (no pink-white glare).
- Red and black while the Eye is open. The epilogue (next night) loses the red completely: cold black and grey, so the
  ending feels like relief (approved).
- Animation and editing in the style of your reference clip: quick cuts, close-ups, whip pans, shake, motion and depth blur, glow,
  colour split on impacts. Glitch flicker kept gentle (easier on the eyes).
- Captions: one word at a time, ALL CAPS, yellow with a black outline.
- Audio: narrator (ElevenLabs Adam, 1.3×) + dreamcore at 50 %. Milo's quoted lines are read by the narrator.
