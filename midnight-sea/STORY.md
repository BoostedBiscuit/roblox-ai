# THE MIDNIGHT SEA · story, bible and look

Original "In this town…" story (IN THIS TOWN master prompt). Vertical 9:16, about 1:10–1:15.
Format: Type E (twist / short thriller), secondary Type A (rule-based mystery). First person. Ending: twist + cliffhanger.

## Stage 2 · Story bible (APPROVED)

| | |
|---|---|
| Town | Hollow Bay: a small fishing town on a black-sand beach |
| Rule | Every night at midnight the sea turns blood-red and everyone walks into it in their sleep. At sunrise they walk back out, dry, remembering nothing. |
| Exception | Nara (narrator, 15) is never asleep at midnight |
| Consequence | Every week one person doesn't reach the water in time. By morning they're gone; only a trail of burned black footprints is left. |
| Goal | Protect her little brother Milo (8), the slowest sleepwalker in town |
| Opposing force | "Whatever takes the late ones", which is Nara herself: at midnight she becomes a shadow with black eyes and burning footprints, and never remembers |
| Stakes | Milo's life |
| Twist | The sea isn't taking people. It's hiding them from her. |
| Clues before the twist | never asleep at midnight · black ash on her hands · the dogs howl toward her · the red water always pulls away from her feet |

## Stage 2 · Script (APPROVED)

1. In this town, every night at midnight, the sea turns red… and everyone walks into it in their sleep.
2. At sunrise, they walk back out. Dry. Remembering nothing.
3. Everyone except me. I've never slept through a single midnight.
4. But every week, one person doesn't reach the water in time.
5. By morning, they're gone. All that's left is a trail of burned, black footprints.
6. And every time, I wake up on the beach… with black ash on my hands.
7. My little brother Milo is the slowest sleepwalker in town.
8. So tonight, I hid under the pier, with my phone recording, to catch whatever takes them.
9. Midnight. The water turned red. The whole town walked in.
10. Except Milo. He was still ten steps from the water.
11. Then every dog in town started howling… right at the pier. Like something was standing right behind me.
12. I ran to push Milo into the sea. But the water pulled away from my feet. It always does.
13. Milo woke up. He looked at me… screamed… and ran into the red water.
14. At sunrise, I watched the video.
15. There was no monster on that beach. Just me. Black eyes. Burning footprints. Chasing my brother.
16. The sea isn't taking them. It's hiding them… from me.
17. And this morning… nobody walked back out.

## Stage 3 · Character and world bible (DRAFT, waiting for approval)

All characters are original blocky Roblox-style avatars built in our own 3D pipeline: square head, block torso,
separate block arms and legs, simple expressive 2D-style faces. No real Roblox assets, logos or third-party characters.

### Characters

**NARA VALE** (protagonist, narrator)
- Age 15. Standard teen avatar proportions (same scale as Rhea in The Reaping Trials).
- Face: large dark-brown eyes with heavy tired lids, thin straight brows, small mouth, faint dark circles under the eyes.
- Hair: long straight black hair in a low ponytail, tied with a red hair tie; loose strands framing the face.
- Clothes: oversized charcoal-grey hoodie (hood down, drawstrings), black jeans, black sneakers with white soles.
- Accessories: black phone with a cracked top-right corner (right hand when recording); thin red string bracelet on the left wrist.
- Palette: charcoal, black, one red accent (hair tie + bracelet).
- Personality: protective, stubborn, exhausted, quietly brave.
- Emotional range: tired-determined → alarmed → desperate → horrified → hollow.
- SHADOW FORM (only seen in the phone video, scenes 15–16): same model and clothes, but the eyes are solid black with a thin
  red glow ring, black ash drifting off her shoulders and hands, and every footprint burns black with a faint red ember glow and smoke.
  She keeps her normal expression only in her own memory; on the video her face is blank.

**MILO VALE** (little brother)
- Age 8. Child avatar, about 0.8× Nara's height, slightly bigger head.
- Face: big round eyes (closed while sleepwalking), freckles on the cheeks, small round mouth.
- Hair: short messy black hair, a cowlick at the back.
- Clothes: red-and-white striped pyjama top, navy pyjama trousers, barefoot.
- Accessory: a small blue plush whale, always in his LEFT hand.
- Personality: sleepy, sweet, trusts his sister completely.
- Emotional range: asleep (eyes closed, slow shuffling steps) → confused waking → terrified scream → running.

**SLEEPWALKERS** (recurring extras: 12–16 townspeople)
- Nightclothes, eyes closed, arms hanging loose, slow even steps, all walking in straight lines toward the sea.
- Three always recognisable:
  - **Old Bram**: fisherman, grey beard, yellow raincoat over pyjamas.
  - **Mrs. Odell**: elderly, long white nightgown, grey hair in a bun.
  - **Mr. Pike**: a dad in a maroon bathrobe and slippers.

**DOGS** (3): blocky dogs (one black, one tan, one grey) on porches and the seawall. They howl with heads tilted up, facing the pier.

### Locations

**HOLLOW BAY BEACH** (main location)
- Layout: a wide black-sand beach curving left to right.
- Landmarks:
  - the long wooden pier on the LEFT, on dark stilts;
  - three old fishing boats pulled up on the sand, centre-right;
  - a low stone seawall with stairs up to the town behind;
  - jagged black rocks on the far right.
- The sea: inky black by day. At midnight it turns glossy blood-red, glowing faintly from below, with black wave crests and a low mist on the water.
- Sky: black, with a large pale-red moon low over the sea (always the same position: above the horizon, right of the pier).
- Fixed details: tide line of foam; driftwood; a red-and-white life ring on the pier rail.

**UNDER THE PIER** (Nara's hiding spot)
- Rows of thick wet stilts with barnacles and seaweed; thin slats of red moonlight falling through the planks above.
- Nara crouches behind the third stilt from the shore, facing the beach.

**THE TOWN ON THE HILL / NARA'S STREET**
- Narrow two-storey wooden fishing houses with peeling dark paint, steep roofs, all windows dark.
- Dim orange-red streetlamps; wooden stairs down to the seawall.
- Nara's house: the last house on the street, nearest the beach; her bedroom window upstairs faces the sea; the dogs gather below it.

**DAWN (ending)**
- The same beach at sunrise: cold grey-red light, the sea calm and dark red, mist; burned footprints on the sand; empty town.

### Props
- **Nara's phone**: on-screen footage look with a REC dot, timestamp 00:00, grain, and a glitch on the reveal.
- **Burned footprints**: black scorch marks in the sand with a faint red ember glow and thin smoke.
- **Black ash**: on Nara's palms; drifting particles.
- **Milo's plush whale**: always in his left hand.

### Look (town visual identity)
- Palette: RED AND BLACK only. Crushed deep blacks, crimson highlights, everything else desaturated. No purple, no blue.
- Light: the red moon as backlight / rim light; red glow from the sea; small warm streetlamps on the hill.
- Atmosphere: low mist over the water, ash particles, light film grain, heavy vignette.
- Animation and editing (from the reference video):
  - a cut every 1–1.5 s;
  - extreme close-ups of eyes and faces, low angles;
  - whip pans with directional blur, shake on scares, zoom punches;
  - glow on red highlights, depth-of-field blur, real motion blur;
  - chromatic aberration on impacts, speed lines when running, a glitch/distortion on the phone-video reveal.
- Captions: one word at a time, lowercase, bold, yellow (#FFE45C) with a black outline, centred.
- Voice: ElevenLabs Adam at 1.3× (same as our other series), about 1,050 characters.
