# THE CORN MAZE · an "In this town…" story (Hollow Bay)

Format: Type E (twist / short thriller), with Type B (survival) influence · ~60–68 s · first person (Nara) · Facebook Reels + YouTube Shorts.
Look: the "In this town" reference style (foggy natural daylight, classic blocky avatars). Captions: one word, ALL CAPS, yellow.

## Stage 1 · Idea (CHOSEN: #7)
"In this town, if you hear your name inside the corn maze, you walk the other way."

## Stage 2 · Story bible (APPROVED)

| | |
|---|---|
| Town | Hollow Bay. The corn maze on Harrow Farm, on the hill above town. |
| Central rule | If you hear your name inside the corn maze, you walk the other way. |
| What everyone believes | The maze grows back overnight every autumn. It is alive and hungry: its walls move, and the voice is the maze luring kids in. |
| The truth (twist) | The maze is a fence. It grows around the old scarecrow in the centre to keep it penned in. The scarecrow is the Caller: it calls you by your given name, in the voice of someone you love. The corn moves to block anyone walking toward it. |
| Exceptions | The corn can't stop you if you crawl under it. The Caller only knows your given name, never the nickname your friends use. |
| Consequence | Kids who reach the scarecrow are never found. |
| Nara's goal | Find her best friend Juno, who went in on a dare and didn't come out. |
| Opposing force | Apparently the maze; really the scarecrow. |
| Stakes | Juno's life, then her own. |
| Ending reveal | The real Juno is safe on the road calling "Vale!". The corn grabs Nara and drags her out. The maze saved them both. |

Fair clues: (1) Juno has always called her "Vale"; the voice says "Nara". (2) The corn slams shut when she runs toward the voice and opens when she turns away. (3) The scarecrow faces the town in the first wide shot, and later always faces Nara. (4) Corn leaves in Juno's hair: the maze pushed him out too.

## Stage 2 · Script (APPROVED; Juno is a boy)
1. In this town, if you hear your name inside the corn maze… you walk the other way.
2. Every autumn it grows back overnight. And every few years, a kid walks in… and never walks out.
3. Everyone says the maze is alive. That it moves.
4. My best friend Juno has called me Vale since we were five. Today he went in on a dare. He didn't come out.
5. So I went in after him. Behind me, the corn closed. The way out was gone.
6. Then, from the fog: "Nara!" Juno's voice.
7. I ran toward it. The corn slammed shut in front of me. Again. And again. But when I turned away… a path opened.
8. The maze was keeping me from him. So I crawled under the stalks.
9. "Nara… over here." In the middle of the maze stood the old scarecrow. Juno's voice was coming out of it.
10. Then, far behind me, from the road: "VALE! Where are you?"
11. Juno never calls me Nara.
12. The scarecrow's head turned. Its arms came off the pole.
13. Then the corn grabbed me. It dragged me back through wall after wall… and dropped me at Juno's feet.
14. The maze was never hungry. It's a fence. And now I know what it's keeping in.

## Stage 3 · Character and world bible (FOR APPROVAL)

### Characters (locked: same model, outfit, hair and proportions in every shot)

**NARA VALE** · 15 · protagonist and narrator (first person)
- Build: classic blocky avatar: one-piece torso 2 × 2 × 1, one-piece arms and legs 1 × 2 × 1, head on the shoulders; about 5.2 studs tall. Hands = skin-coloured ends of the arms; shoes painted on the legs.
- Face (beautiful): big almond eyes, warm amber irises with two highlights, winged upper lash line with lashes, thin arched black brows, small nose, soft rose lips, light pink blush.
- Hair: black; side-swept bangs falling to her right, long locks framing her face, low ponytail with a red hair tie.
- Clothing: oversized charcoal hoodie (white drawstrings, kangaroo pocket, ribbed hem), black jeans, black sneakers with white soles.
- Accessories: thin red string bracelet on her LEFT wrist.
- Palette: charcoal, black, warm skin, red accents.
- Personality: steady, protective, acts fast, stubborn when someone she loves is in danger.
- Emotional range: determined → worried → scared → shocked → eyes squeezed shut → tearful relief.

**JUNO** · 15 · boy · Nara's best friend
- Build: the same classic blocky avatar as Nara.
- Face (handsome): sharper hazel-green eyes, straighter dark brows, a faint eyelid crease, small nose, confident half-smile.
- Hair: messy dark brown, volume on top brushed back, bangs falling across his forehead to one side, short sides.
- Clothing: bright yellow raincoat (mustard trim, four toggles, two pockets, hood down), dark slate T-shirt, blue jeans, dark sneakers with white soles.
- Accessories: none. At the end, corn leaves caught in his hair.
- Palette: yellow, slate, denim blue.
- Personality: daring, joking, a little reckless. Has called her "Vale" since they were five, never "Nara".
- Emotional range: half-smile → smug (the dare) → panicked shouting (on the road) → relieved.

**THE SCARECROW (the Caller)** · ageless · the real threat
- Build: unlike the humans: very tall (about 1.6 × Nara standing), long thin arms and legs, long straw fingers.
- Face: burlap sack with two ragged stitched eye holes (empty when still, tiny pale pupils when awake) and a long crooked stitched mouth that splits open when it speaks.
- Hair: straw tufts under the hat and at the neck.
- Clothing: floppy dark felt hat with a torn brim, long patched slate-grey coat with a torn hem and dark buttons, burlap trousers tied with rope at the ankles.
- Accessories: rope collar; straw poking out of the cuffs and ankles.
- Palette: burlap tan, slate grey, straw yellow, dark brown.
- Behaviour: hangs on its tall wooden cross-pole in the centre clearing, head drooping. It can only speak given names, in a loved one's voice (here, Juno's). Its head is always turned toward whoever is in the maze.
- Range: still → awake (pupils appear) → speaking (mouth splits) → arms come off the pole, it steps down.

**THE MAZE** (behaves like a character)
- The corn moves: stalks shiver, then bend and knit together to close a path ("slams shut"), or part to open one.
- In the rescue, stalks wrap around Nara like hands and pass her from wall to wall, out to the road.

**Extras:** none on screen. The missing kids appear only as faded flyers.

### Locations (locked)

**Harrow Farm corn maze** (on the hill above Hollow Bay)
- Layout: a square maze about 34 m across (10 × 10 cells, 3.4 m between walls), with dry corn walls about 1.3 × Nara's height. Entrance on the south side, exit on the north side to the road, and a 2 × 2-cell clearing in the middle with the scarecrow's tall wooden cross-pole.
- Landmarks:
  - At the entrance: a wooden sign reading "HARROW FARM · CORN MAZE", hay bales, pumpkins, and faded missing-kid flyers on the sign posts.
  - In the centre: the cross-pole.
  - Far off in the fog: a red barn to the north-west and bare trees around the field.
- Materials: golden-tan dry corn with a little green; packed brown dirt paths with straw bits, darker soil under the walls.
- Lighting: a foggy autumn afternoon with soft overcast light and soft shadows. Pale grey-blue fog swallows everything beyond about 10 m, and low mist drifts between the walls. Natural colours, no colour filter.
- Fixed: the time of day never changes; the fog stays the same density throughout.

**The road** (north edge)
- Layout: a gravel road with a wooden rail fence, where the maze exit opens onto it. This is where the real Juno stands at the end.
- Lighting: the same fog.
